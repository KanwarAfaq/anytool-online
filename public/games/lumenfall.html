About Lumenfall

Lumenfall is a free 3D adventure that runs in your browser. Explore Crystal Valley, fight enemies, find nine energy crystals and reach the final portal. It is built with Three.js, React and a physics engine, and everything you see is generated in your browser.

Lumenfall needs a keyboard and mouse, so it is built for desktop and laptop browsers.

Tips

  • Collect all nine glowing crystals to open the final portal.
  • Watch your health bar. Enemies hit harder as you go deeper into the realm.
  • Check the mini-map in the corner to find crystals you have missed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play Lumenfall on my phone?

Lumenfall uses a keyboard and mouse with pointer lock, so it works best on a desktop or laptop in a current browser.

What do I need to run Lumenfall?

A recent browser with WebGL 2. The first load downloads a few megabytes of game code and the character model.
