THE EXPERIENCE

Four friendly young runners compete in a colorful 1,500-meter grand prix. Watch their animated legs and arms, glide between four running lanes, leap over striped hurdles, and collect shining stars and energy boosts. Your finishing position determines your score.

HOW TO PLAY

Move lanes with ← / → or A / D, jump with Space / W / ↑, and hold Shift to sprint. Use on-screen controls on phones. Press P to pause.

Park Dash FAQ

How do you play Park Dash?

Race on foot against three kid rivals. Jump hurdles, dodge puddles, collect stars and sprint to the finish.

Are there cars or scooters?

No. Park Dash is only a running race between kids on foot.
