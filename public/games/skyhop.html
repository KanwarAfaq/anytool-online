Tips

About Skyhop

Skyhop is a free one-tap flying game for your browser. Flap through gaps, collect stars and fly from sunrise into the night. Tap, click or press Space to flap.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Skyhop?

Tap, click or press Space to flap. Fly through the gaps, collect stars and avoid the obstacles.

Is Skyhop free?

Yes. It is free, runs in your browser and needs no download or sign-up.
