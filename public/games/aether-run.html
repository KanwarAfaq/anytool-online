EXIT TO ARCADE

THE EXPERIENCE

Pilot a responsive anti-gravity craft through a luminous skyline. Crimson barriers drain your ship's energy. Aqua rings recharge it. Gold gates reward precision. Boost trades energy for speed.

HOW TO PLAY

Use A / D or ← / → to steer, and hold Shift or ↑ to boost. Press P to pause. On touchscreens use the on-screen direction and boost buttons.
