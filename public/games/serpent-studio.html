THE EXPERIENCE

Classic challenges you to avoid walls and your tail. Zen wraps the board and lets you recover from mistakes. Sprint gives you 90 seconds to score. Maze adds stone barriers to navigate. Four glowing skins, three speed settings, bonus fruit and mode-specific high scores make every round different.

HOW TO PLAY

Choose your mode, speed and snake skin before starting. Steer with Arrow keys / WASD, swipe the board or use touch arrows. Eat the glowing fruit to grow. Press P to pause. Press Space to start or resume.
