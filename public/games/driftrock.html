Tips

  • Your ship keeps drifting, so thrust in short bursts and use the screen edges to wrap around.
  • You earn an extra life every 5,000 points and a bonus for clearing each wave.

About Driftrock

Driftrock is a free neon asteroid shooter for your browser. Fly, shoot and survive waves of splitting rocks. Play with the keyboard, mouse or touch. Turn with A and D, thrust with W and shoot with Space.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Driftrock?

Turn with A and D or the arrow keys, thrust with W and hold Space to shoot. Every rock you hit splits into smaller pieces. Clear them all to start the next wave.

Does Driftrock work on a phone?

Yes. Press and hold on the screen to aim and fire, and hold away from the ship to thrust.
